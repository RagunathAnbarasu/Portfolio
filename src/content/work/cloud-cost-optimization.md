## The challenge

Cloud usage across AWS and Azure represented a substantial recurring operational cost. Improving that footprint required collaboration with the engineering teams using those platforms. The opportunity was to connect cloud efficiency with the ongoing work of maintaining and improving infrastructure and software delivery.

## My contribution

As a Senior Software Developer in DevOps at OpenText, I partnered with engineering teams to optimize cloud usage across AWS and Azure. The work combined platform engineering with a focus on the recurring costs of operating cloud infrastructure.

This built on earlier experience at Micro Focus, where I developed cleanup automation for failed deployments, dangling resources, and unused infrastructure. That earlier work reduced cloud operational costs by approximately 30%. The two outcomes came from different roles and periods; they are separate improvements rather than a combined savings figure.

## The result

The AWS and Azure optimization work reduced monthly cloud spending by approximately 69%. This percentage represents the change between the before-and-after monthly spending figures supplied in my resume.

The experience reinforced the value of treating cloud cost optimization as an engineering collaboration: a platform concern that benefits from working directly with the teams delivering software.
