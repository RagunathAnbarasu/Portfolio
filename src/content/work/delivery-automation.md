## The challenge

Software delivery depends on more than a deployment pipeline. Engineering teams need timely pull request validation, consistent infrastructure checks, reliable artifact publishing, and repeatable release workflows. My work across two DevOps roles focused on reducing friction in those parts of the delivery process.

## My contribution

As a Software Developer in DevOps at OpenText, from February 2023 to August 2025, I built automation using Jenkins, GitLab CI, and Python. It supported AWS infrastructure validation and artifact publishing, accelerating pull request validation by approximately 4× and improving deployment readiness.

In my Senior Software Developer role, beginning in August 2025, I implemented automated deployment pipelines for high-priority initiatives. I also improved pipeline reliability through failure notifications, health monitoring, and remediation coordination, and partnered with engineering teams to standardize deployment and release workflows.

## The result

The earlier role delivered approximately 4× faster PR validation. The senior role reduced deployment effort to approximately one-third of the previous effort, alongside a reduction in deployment time.

These outcomes measure different parts of delivery and belong to different periods. Together, they reflect my focus on practical automation that improves developer experience and supports more predictable software delivery.
