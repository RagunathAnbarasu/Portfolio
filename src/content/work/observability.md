## The challenge

Kubernetes workloads needed useful visibility into cluster health and service telemetry. At the same time, metrics volume and time-series database storage usage were areas for improvement. The work focused on making observability more effective while reducing the overhead associated with collecting and storing telemetry.

## My contribution

As a Software Developer in DevOps at OpenText, I implemented and optimized an OpenTelemetry-based observability setup for Kubernetes workloads. The work used OpenTelemetry collectors, Prometheus, and Grafana to support visibility into services and cluster health.

I worked across the telemetry collection, monitoring, and visualization stack. This connected improvements in the observability setup with the engineering teams' need to understand their running systems. The aim was a monitoring platform that offered useful operational signals with a more manageable metrics footprint.

## The result

Metrics volume decreased by approximately 10×, and TSDB storage usage decreased by approximately 5×. These are separate measures: the reduction in collected metrics and the reduction in time-series storage consumption.

The improvements also increased visibility into Kubernetes cluster health and service telemetry. This experience now informs my work across observability and platform engineering, alongside my OpenTelemetry and Prometheus certifications.
